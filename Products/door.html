01 • PLY DOOR

Plywood Doors

Plywood doors are made from bonded wood-veneer layers. The right plywood grade gives a strong, stable door with good screw-holding and a wide choice of laminate, veneer or paint finishes. For moisture-heavy locations, BWR/BWP grades are preferable to ordinary MR plywood.

Layered plywood construction Strong & good screw holding Laminate, veneer & paint finishes BWR/BWP grades for wetter areas

Best suited for: bedrooms, living rooms, offices and main/interior doors. Choose the plywood grade according to the location — standard interior plywood is not the same as BWP/marine-grade plywood.

02 • FRP DOOR

FRP Doors Fiber Reinforced Plastic

A practical option for moisture-prone locations where low maintenance matters.

FRP doors are made using fiberglass-reinforced plastic. Indian FRP door manufacturers commonly highlight resistance to water, termites, insects, corrosion, swelling and warping, with smooth, matte or woodgrain/veneer-style finishes available.

Waterproof / water resistant Termite & insect resistant Corrosion & weather resistant Low maintenance

Best suited for: bathrooms, utility areas, humid rooms, commercial and industrial spaces. Your current FRP collection is listed at ₹6,000.

03 • WPC DOOR

WPC Doors

WPC (Wood Plastic Composite) doors use a wood-fibre/polymer composite instead of a conventional timber core. Manufacturers position WPC for moisture-heavy areas because the material is designed to resist water absorption, termites, fungal decay and warping.

Waterproof / moisture resistant Termite & borer resistant Suitable for humid climates Low maintenance

Best suited for: bathrooms, wash areas, kitchens, humid rooms, commercial interiors and locations where water and termites are a concern.

04 • HDHMR DOOR

HDHMR Doors

HDHMR means High Density High Moisture Resistance. It is a dense engineered wood-fibre board with a uniform core and moisture-resistant resins. Its smooth, dense surface is well suited to laminate, paint, veneer and CNC routing.

High moisture resistance Borer & termite resistance CNC-friendly smooth surface Paint, laminate & veneer finishes

Best suited for: bedrooms, kitchens, wardrobes, interior doors and humid interiors where a smooth premium finish is wanted. Important: HDHMR is moisture-resistant, not a claim of unlimited waterproofing; edges should be properly sealed.
