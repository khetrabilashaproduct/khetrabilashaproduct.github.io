Our Story

From local work to products people can rely on.

At Khetra Bilasha Cement Products, we focus on making practical, durable products with a strong emphasis on quality. What started around local manufacturing has grown into a business serving customers with doors, hardware, plywood and cement products.

We believe good products should not need a complicated story: solid materials, careful work and clear communication with customers.
